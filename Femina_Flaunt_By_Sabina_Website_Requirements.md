# Femina Flaunt By Sabina --- Premium Website Product & Design Requirements

> **Document purpose:** Master requirements document for designing and
> developing the official website of **Femina Flaunt By Sabina**.
>
> **Primary objective:** Create a premium, editorial, conversion-focused
> digital home that turns existing Google, Instagram, Facebook, and
> campaign traffic into appointment requests while strengthening the
> brand's luxury positioning.
>
> **Document status:** Discovery / pre-design specification\
> **Prepared from:** The supplied prospect dossier, with design/product
> recommendations explicitly marked as recommendations.\
> **Important:** Business facts that require owner confirmation are not
> to be presented as verified website content until approved.

# 3. Project Goals

## 3.1 Primary goals

1.  Establish an official premium digital presence.
2.  Make the brand look significantly more premium and trustworthy.
3.  Convert social and Google traffic into appointment requests.
4.  Make services easy to understand.
5.  Showcase bridal services prominently.
6.  Showcase premium/advanced treatments without making unsupported
    medical claims.
7.  Build trust through reviews, founder story, expertise, hygiene, and
    visual quality.
8.  Make contact and booking extremely easy on mobile.
9.  Strengthen local SEO visibility for Gulshan-2 and Dhaka.
10. Provide a scalable platform for future services, offers, campaigns,
    and content.

## 3.2 Secondary goals

-   Improve perceived brand value.
-   Reduce repetitive questions through clear service information.
-   Make Google/social traffic land on a professional owned platform.
-   Give the marketing agency a high-quality landing destination.
-   Create reusable campaign landing pages.
-   Establish consistent brand language.
-   Make future online booking automation possible.

## 3.3 What the website must NOT feel like

Avoid:

-   Generic salon template
-   Cheap beauty-parlour aesthetic
-   Overuse of pink
-   Excessive gradients
-   Cartoonish icons
-   Cluttered service cards
-   Overloaded navigation
-   Excessive animations
-   Stock-photo-heavy design
-   Fake luxury
-   Aggressive sales language
-   Unverified medical promises

-   Champagne

-   Deep brown
-   Warm beige

## 6.2 Typography

### Heading font

Recommended characteristics:

-   High-contrast serif
-   Elegant
-   Fashion/editorial
-   Strong large-display presence

### Body font

Recommended characteristics:

-   Modern sans-serif
-   Excellent readability
-   Clean
-   Professional
-   Good Bangla/English fallback support if Bangla content is introduced

### Typography hierarchy

Use a clear scale:

``` text
Display
↓
H1
↓
H2
↓
H3
↓
Eyebrow / Label
↓
Body
↓
Caption
↓
Microcopy
```

Do not use too many font families.

Recommended maximum:

-   1 display serif
-   1 body sans-serif

# 21. Appointment Booking System

This is the website's primary conversion mechanism.

## 21.1 Booking form

Required fields:

-   Full Name
-   Phone Number
-   WhatsApp Number
-   Service
-   Preferred Date
-   Preferred Time
-   Message / Notes

Optional:

-   Email
-   Preferred staff member
-   First-time visitor checkbox
-   Bridal/event date
-   How did you hear about us?

## 21.2 Service selector

Example:

``` text
Select a service

Hair
Skin
Bridal Makeup
Makeup
Grooming
Exosome Therapy
Glutathione IV Therapy
Wellness
Body Contouring
Other
```

Final list must be supplied by client.

## 21.3 Date/time

The initial MVP should treat this as a **booking request**, not
guaranteed real-time availability.

Example:

> Your requested time will be confirmed by our team.

Unless a real-time scheduling backend is implemented.

## 21.4 Submission

On submission:

1.  Validate fields.
2.  Display confirmation.
3.  Send booking notification to salon.
4.  Store booking request securely if a backend is used.
5.  Optionally notify customer.
6.  Provide WhatsApp/phone fallback.

## 21.5 Confirmation message

Example:

> Thank you. Your appointment request has been received. Our team will
> contact you shortly to confirm your preferred date and time.
