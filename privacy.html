Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page is a draft prepared from the project requirements and must be approved by the business owner before launch.

What we collect

When you submit an appointment request through this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email (optional), preferred date and time, selected service, and any notes you choose to share.

Why we collect it

The information is used solely to contact you and confirm your appointment request. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand which services clients are interested in.

Where it is stored and who can access it

Booking requests are handled by the salon team. Access is limited to staff who need it to confirm appointments. Details of storage location and retention will be documented before launch, in line with the data-security requirements of this project.

How long we keep it

We retain booking information only as long as needed to manage appointments and as required for business records. Retention periods will be finalized before launch.

Your choices

You may request to update or delete the personal information you submitted by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use analytics to understand site usage. Any analytics or cookie disclosure will be finalized and disclosed before launch.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the details on the Contact page.
